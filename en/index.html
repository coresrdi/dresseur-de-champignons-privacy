Privacy policy

App: Le dresseur de champignons
Privacy lead: Jeff Gravel (CORES RDI)
Email: coresrdi@gmail.com
Effective date: 24 November 2026

This policy describes how personal information is handled by the mobile app Le dresseur de champignons, published by Jeff Gravel, doing business as CORES RDI, in Quebec (Canada). It is separate from any policy that applies to another CORES RDI product.

1. No data is collected

The app does not collect any personal information. It does not create an account, does not ask for an email address or a name, and does not send any data to CORES RDI, to Google, or to any other third party.

In the current distributed build (package com.coresrdi.dresseurdechampignons):

  • no Android permission is requested (no Internet, camera, location, or external storage);
  • the app makes no network calls;
  • there are no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting, no in-app purchases, and no online game services.

2. Local saves only

Progress (rotating save slots and game state) is stored only on the device, in the app’s private storage. Those files do not leave the device. They are under the control of the person who uses the device; uninstalling the app deletes them.

CORES RDI cannot access these saves and does not keep any copy of them.

3. Quebec Law 25

Quebec’s Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25) applies to enterprises in Quebec. Because CORES RDI does not collect, hold, use, or disclose any personal information through this app, there is no file to access, correct, or delete with us.

The person responsible for the protection of personal information is Jeff Gravel (CORES RDI). For questions about this policy, write to coresrdi@gmail.com.

If you believe your rights have not been respected, you may also contact the Commission d’accès à l’information du Québec.

4. Children

The app is intended for ages 13 and up. It does not collect information from children or from anyone else.

5. Changes

If a future update were to collect data, request a permission, or connect to a network, this policy would be updated before that app update, and the date above would be revised.
